# Climate Data Players

A knowledge graph of the people and organizations stewarding U.S. climate and Earth-observation data.

**Live site:** [sdiggs.github.io/climate-data-players](https://sdiggs.github.io/climate-data-players/)

The graph maps actors across the six-role stewardship lifecycle: **produce, preserve, activate, coordinate, advocate, train.** It is intended as a navigable reference for journalists, researchers, philanthropic partners, and members of the public who want to understand who is actually doing the work, where, and how the work connects.

This explorer is part of the Atlas layer of the [Keeling Curve Foundation's](https://keelingcurve.org) Vital Signs 2.0 initiative.
