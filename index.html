Click any node to inspect. Hover for a summary — the rest of the network fades while you do.

Round nodes are organizations active in stewardship. Dark rectangles are federal producers — the agencies that generate the underlying data the ecosystem orbits.

What this shows. 38 organizations and 11 federal data producers in the climate and environmental stewardship ecosystem. Solid ties are explicit "coordinates with" relationships between orgs (49). Dashed ties (76) point from an org to the federal producer whose data it explicitly works with.

The peer-initiative frame. KCF's Data Continuity Initiative (DCI) anchors the producer end — sustaining the observations themselves. NYCE's Data Stewardship Initiative (DSI) anchors the access end — governance, usability, public-facing tools. The preservation cohort in between (PEDP, Data Rescue Project, ESIP, ICPSR/DataLumos, Harvard LIL, Source Cooperative) needs a shared prioritization spine. Brass-ringed nodes are the KCF inner orbit: orgs actively coordinating with DCI on producer/continuity work.
