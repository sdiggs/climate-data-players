About

Click any node to inspect. Hover for a summary — the rest of the network fades while you do.

Round nodes are organizations active in stewardship. Dark rectangles are federal producers — the agencies that generate the underlying data the ecosystem orbits. Smaller faded dots are partner-tier contributors (technical partners assisting primary orgs).

What this shows. 46 organizations (45 primary + 1 partner-tier) and 11 federal data producers in the climate and environmental stewardship ecosystem. Solid ties are explicit "coordinates with" relationships between orgs (65). Dashed ties (88) point from an org to the federal producer whose data it explicitly works with.

Parent-initiative structure (v1.1). Several initiatives now show their parent organization in the detail panel: PEDP under OEDP, SHIP under AGCI, Climate Data Collaborative under Data Foundation, Earth Obs Workshop under NASEM, Climate Program Portal under Atlas Public Policy, Silencing Science Tracker under Sabin Center, Source Cooperative under Radiant Earth Foundation. Universities and other institutional hosts (Harvard, Stanford, Internet Archive, etc.) appear as label-only parents without their own nodes.

The peer-initiative frame. KCF's Data Continuity Initiative (DCI) anchors the producer end — sustaining the observations themselves. NYCE's Data Stewardship Initiative (DSI) anchors the access end — governance, usability, public-facing tools. Brass-ringed nodes are the KCF inner orbit.

Detail panel depth (new). Clicking PEDP now surfaces 9 specific federal dataset replicas (CEJST, EJScreen, HIFLD Next, CDC SVI/EJI, DOE Local Investments, FEMA Future Risk, EPA EJ Grants Map, EPA Science Hub, plus the archived data portfolio) along with the Data + Screening Tools system and funder attribution. OEDP shows all 7 of its internal initiatives.
