Click any node to inspect. Hover for a quick summary.

Round nodes are organizations active in stewardship. Dark rectangles are federal producers — the agencies that generate the underlying data the ecosystem orbits.

What this shows. 38 organizations and 11 federal data producers in the climate and environmental stewardship ecosystem. Solid ties are explicit "coordinates with" relationships between orgs (49). Dashed ties (76) point from an org to the federal producer whose data it explicitly works with.

The peer-initiative frame. KCF's Data Continuity Initiative (DCI) anchors the producer end — sustaining the observations themselves. NYCE's Data Stewardship Initiative (DSI) anchors the access end — governance, usability, public-facing tools. The preservation cohort in between (PEDP, Data Rescue Project, ESIP, ICPSR/DataLumos, Harvard LIL, Source Cooperative) needs a shared prioritization spine. The brass-ringed nodes are the KCF inner orbit: orgs actively coordinating with DCI on producer/continuity work.

Why federal producers are anchors. NOAA, NASA, USGS, NSF, EPA, USDA, DOE, NIST, FEMA, ONR, DOI aren't orgs to "recruit" into the stewardship ecosystem — they're the agencies generating the data the ecosystem exists to preserve and activate. Showing them as anchor nodes makes that gravity visible, and the dashed dependency edges make the data-flow picture explicit.
